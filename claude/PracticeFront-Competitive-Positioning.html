vs Dental Intelligence

DI gives you 47 metrics and expects you to study them. We give you 3 traffic lights and say "here's who can fix the red one." Their $85M round funds more complexity. Our approach exploits the innovator's dilemma — simplification would cannibalize their premium pricing.

vs Sikka

Sikka connects 400+ PMS systems and just launched MCP server support for AI agents. They're the best pipe in the market. We're the dashboard that makes the data meaningful AND the marketplace that connects it to action. Different layer, potentially complementary.

vs Kolla

Kolla solves the plumbing problem at $19/location. We solve the meaning problem (what does this data tell you?) and the action problem (what do you do about it?). Kolla could become an integration partner for multi-PMS coverage, not a competitor.

vs Adit

Adit tries to replace everything — comms, scheduling, payments, analytics. High switching cost, forces PMS migration. We monitor everything without replacing anything. Zero switching cost. We work alongside existing tools, not against them.
