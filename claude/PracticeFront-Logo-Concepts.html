Recommendation

03 — Blocks

Blocks is the strongest because it's the simplest. Three overlapping rectangles read as "data layers" instantly, the opacity gradient creates depth with zero complexity, and it stays fully legible down to 16px. It also animates naturally — three layers sliding into position with staggered spring easing on page load.

02 (Grid) works well as a secondary pattern element — repeatable across backgrounds, loading states, and section dividers on the website.
